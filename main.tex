\textbf{Summary.}
State the central question and why it matters. Describe your approach in one or two
sentences. Report the primary findings, emphasizing significance, generality, novelty,
and relevance to the Cosyne audience. Close with a sentence on broader impact, e.g.,

\textbf{Background and question.}
Define the question and why it is important and unsolved. If you cite your own prior
work, write it in the third person: ``Author et al.~\cite{ref1} showed\ldots'', never
